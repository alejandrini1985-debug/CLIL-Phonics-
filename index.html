Methodological Foundations

What is CLIL?

CLIL (Content and Language Integrated Learning) is an approach where students learn curricular content (like science, math, or art) while simultaneously developing their English language skills. The language becomes the vehicle to explore the world.

  • Content: Acquiring new knowledge.
  • Communication: Using English to learn.
  • Cognition: Critical thinking.
  • Culture: Global citizenship.

Systematic Phonics

A systematic approach to teaching literacy that focuses on the direct relationship between spoken sounds (phonemes) and the letters that represent them (graphemes).

Children are taught to blend sounds to read CVC (Consonant-Vowel-Consonant) words and segment them to spell. This guarantees fluency, correct pronunciation, and confidence from an early age, avoiding visual memorization of whole words.

The Role of Artificial Intelligence (AI)

AI acts as your pedagogical assistant. We will use it to automate lesson plans, adapt complex texts to A1/A2 levels, design illustrated decodable books, and generate analytical rubrics in seconds, allowing you to focus on what matters most: your students.
